Practical ventilation and ductwork guides

These are the measurements, checks and distinctions that matter when ductwork and fire dampers move from a drawing to an actual opening, ceiling void or plantroom.

Built around real site decisions

VentTools guides are not written to pad out search results. Each one supports a working calculator or a recurring installation problem: measuring a rolled offset, identifying the correct bend take-off, keeping a replacement duct size practical, or separating a fire damper's nominal duct from its casing and structural opening.

General ductwork guidance is based on site experience and transparent geometry. Manufacturer-specific life-safety information is kept separate and must be checked against the current installation manual, approved project detail and fire strategy.
