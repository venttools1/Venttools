ABOUT VENTTOOLS

Built for real ductwork installation

VentTools is an independent collection of HVAC calculators and installation-reference tools designed around the measurements and decisions that arise on site.

What VentTools does

VentTools turns published manufacturer information into clearer calculation steps, opening dimensions and practical installation notes. It also provides general ductwork tools such as the offset calculator and round / rectangular duct converter.

Independent by design

VentTools is not owned, approved or endorsed by any manufacturer shown on the website. Manufacturer names and product references are used only to help users select and verify the relevant published installation method.

Built from site experience

The tools are based on practical ductwork installation problems encountered on working sites. Installation photographs shown on VentTools are original site photographs supplied by the site owner, not generic stock images. They illustrate the type of work behind the calculators but are never used as substitutes for approved drawings or manufacturer instructions.

Help improve it

Corrections and suggestions are welcome at info@venttools.com.
